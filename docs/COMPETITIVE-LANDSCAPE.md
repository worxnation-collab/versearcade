## The answer: Ascend: Bible Game (Pharah LLC)

Not the biggest app in the category — the only one building the same product for
the same player.

In **June 2026 their v3.0 added a phoenix companion with evolution stages, a
personal den you decorate, collectible relics, gift exchange between friends'
dens, a prayer hub and co-op group studies.** That is our 1.2.0 — the Upper Room,
gifts, relics, companions and prayer — shipped two months ago by somebody else.

## Where they are soft — and the clock on it

Their 4.8 store rating is a prompt average. Written reviews split 242/41/47/41/176
across 5★→1★: a third of everyone who typed something typed a one-star. The themes
are the energy wall (crashes at zero hearts, ads-for-hearts not crediting),
"advertised free like Duolingo but unusable after signup without paying", the
$9.99 price, and questions that misquote the verse they test.

**That is a monetisation problem, not a quality problem — and their team has said
publicly they hope to go freemium in late 2026 / early 2027.** If they do, our
loudest advantage disappears and differentiation narrows to the church layer and
the no-shame doctrine. Re-run this comparison in Q4 2026.

## The rest of the field, by directness

- **Tier 0 — YouVersion.** 1B installs (Nov 2025), ~17.5m DAU, 11.2m installs/mo.
  Already has streaks, badges, friend-shared plans. Not a rival; the ground.
- **Tier 2 — Versle** (3.6k ratings, 4.9★, seven modes, active) owns the shared
  daily drop but has no world, avatar or church layer. **Bible Way** is the closest
  on paper (XP, 50+ unlockable heroes, 100+ badges, "Bible Clubs") but has 172
  ratings and hasn't shipped since Mar 2025. **Manna** is the AI-mentor take.
- **Tier 3 — Daily Bible Trivia** (Super Lucky Games). 91k ratings, top of the
  Bible-games charts. Coins, gems, ads, $6.99/wk, hint currency, piggy bank; no
  streak, no leaderboard, no social. Beats us on volume, competes for a different
  intent. An ASO obstacle, not a rival.
- **Tier 4 — Glorify** (20m+ downloads, $40m a16z Series A), **Hallow**,
  **Pray.com**. Same morning minute, same subscription dollar, different mechanics.

## What nobody owns

1. **Playing for your congregation.** 40+ apps surveyed; church platforms gamify
   their own members and Bible games have friend groups, but nothing connects a
   public daily game to the actual building you attend.
2. **A game with no loser.** Searching the review press for shame-free,
   non-comparative Bible gaming returns nothing. Every gamified competitor runs
   hearts, energy, streak-loss or a leaderboard you fall down.
3. **Content without a submission.** The catalog overlay + prepacked quest verbs
   ship a season as data. Ascend ships daily but ships *builds*.
4. **Owning the whole Bible.** 31,102 slots filling in as a possession, offline.

## What follows

1. **Ship 1.2.0.** The feature set is at parity or ahead nearly everywhere. The
   problem is that a competitor shipped the same ideas in June and updates daily
   while our best work has never been uploaded. No positioning fixes that.
2. **Lead the listing with the three things Ascend cannot say** — no energy meter,
   nothing for sale, a wrong answer teaches you something. The promo text already
   carries "no shame, no pop quiz energy"; it belongs in the description's first
   line, not its fifth section.
3. **Take the church wedge in ASO.** "church" currently sits among thirteen generic
   keywords. Congregation-shaped intent is uncontested by every app surveyed.
4. **Do not copy the phoenix.** Evolution stages, a battle pass and purchasable
   relics are what generate their one-star reviews. Depth for our pets comes from
   where they walk, not from what they cost.
