# The Leader's Desk — formats, scripts, recording sheet

On-screen tag: **From the Leader's Desk**. Posts Tue + Thu, 12:30 ET (the
exchange keeps Mon/Wed/Fri), YouTube + Facebook only.

Caption note on EVERY post (an `aiNote` branch of its own, never inherited):

## The formats (voice + church art only)

| # | Format | Visual sequence | What the voice does |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | **The week, laid out** | Series header → 3–5 verse cards for that week, one cut per day (card enters, 2% push, cut on a cue word) → building end card | Says what the series is for and what each day is doing |
| B | **Draft → final** | The draft version with a plain **DRAFT** label, held → a cross-fade or flip to the approved version labelled **APPROVED** → that card alone, full frame | Says what changed and why — the friction moment |
| C | **One card, one reason** | One card, held for the whole post: a slow push, then the text revealed line by line | Why this verse is on this day for these people |
| D | **The notice** | Notice card(s). Usually two: the long first version, then the short final | How to announce something without it reading like a bulletin |
| E | **Leader to leader** | 3–4 cards cut on the beat as B-roll, no labels | One principle, said plainly, ending on a question |
| F | **Planning ahead** | Series header → a row of 7 blank day tiles filling in as the first cards are drawn → building end card | Thinking ahead out loud: what's coming, and why |

Labels (DRAFT / APPROVED / day names) are text over the art, not drawings of
the app. Nothing in a frame is drawn to look like a screen.

## Recording sheet

**One session, about 25 minutes**, in this order. Similar tones sit together
so your voice doesn't swing:

1. **Warm-up / dry runs:** read Take 1 twice without keeping it.
2. **Plain and explanatory:** Takes 1, 4, 9.
3. **Wry, a small smile:** Takes 2, 5, 7. These are the friction ones. The
   hook is a confession, not a complaint.
4. **Quiet and slow:** Takes 3, 6, 8, 10. Drop a little in pace and volume.
   Leave real air at `//`.
5. **Hooks again:** re-read all ten **[HOOK]** lines on their own, as
   "Take one hook", "Take two hook"… The hook decides distribution, and a
   second try is cheap.

Performance notes:
- **About 140 words a minute.** Slower than feels natural. Each take is
  100–140 words, so 40–55 seconds. Take 10 is about 20.
- **Talk to one leader across a desk**, not to an audience. No "hey guys",
  no "so today".
- **Don't land the last line of a hook.** Let it hang a little, so the body
  sounds like the answer.
- **The friction lines are the most important ones** ("That was the
  problem", "So I wrote my own", "I threw it out"). Say them flat. Don't
  perform them.
- **CTAs are throwaways.** Same energy as the line before, never brighter.
  If one sounds like an ad, re-read it more quietly.
- **A fluff mid-line:** pause, then start that SENTENCE again. Don't restart
  the take.
- **Do not ad-lib a number** ("took me five minutes", "a dozen people"). A
  number in the voice has to be checked against real data, and these weren't.
- Same room, same mic distance as your verse hooks, so the series sounds like
  one person.

## Flags: hard to automate, or risks looking fake

1. **The draft has to be captured BEFORE it's overwritten.** `church_verses`
   keeps only the current row, so once Wednesday's note is replaced, the
   engine has no "before" to show. Snapshot the `church-draft` response for
   the target dates when they're drafted. **Fail closed:** no snapshot, or a
   draft identical to the final, and the Draft → final post skips its day
   (the exchange's rule).
2. **Every post checks its own claims before it renders.** The series exists
   on those dates, the referenced days are approved and fresh, Thursday Nov 26
   is discarded, the reorder happened. Anything missing → skip, never post a
   voice describing a thing the data doesn't show. This is the
   "voiced"/"opened" lesson again: the claim travels from the data, never
   from the script.
3. **The cards must be RE-RENDERED from the real rows**, not baked from this
   sheet. If Psalm 100:4's BSB text or a note is edited later, the post shows
   what members saw.
4. **Cuts timed to cue words** ("Monday", "so I", "Thursday") need the Whisper
   alignment `tiktokAlign` already does. A cut a second late on a held card is
   the thing that makes it read as a slideshow.
5. **The tile row (Format F) and the side-by-side swap (Take 7) are new
   layouts.** Everything else is one card or a sequence of cards. If you want
   the smallest first PR, ship A, B (single flip), C, D, E, and the two F
   takes when the tile layout exists. The bank still covers four weeks.
6. **Repetition is the main fake-looking risk with no screen footage.** Ten
   posts of cards gently pushing in will feel templated by week three. The
   checks that keep it from that: no two consecutive posts share a format,
   no template repeats within four posts, and the B posts must actually show
   a difference you can see.
7. **"Thought of you with this one."** is the product's real default line
   (0138). If the default changes, Take 8 is wrong; check before it posts.
8. **The building end card has to be the congregation's real tier and skin**
   (`church_json`), not the nicest render. A grand cathedral under "a very
   small church" is the one frame a pastor would catch.
9. **Disclosure accuracy:** the note says the card ART is AI. If a future
   format uses any of your own photos, it needs a different line.
10. **It's a third post on the networks that judge a channel.** Two a week is
    light, but watch `analytics` on the verse and story posts in the same
    weeks. If they dip, this track is the first suspect.
