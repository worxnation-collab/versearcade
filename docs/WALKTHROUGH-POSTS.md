# The walkthrough: what to paste where

Two files, one cut:

- `Verse-Arcade-Walkthrough-YouTube-16x9.mp4`: landscape, for YouTube. A
  vertical video under 3 minutes is filed as a **Short**, and a Short's
  description link can't be tapped, which is the whole job of this video
  (`docs/YOUTUBE-FUNNEL.md`). Landscape makes it long-form.
- `Verse-Arcade-Walkthrough-v5-HD.mp4`: vertical, for Facebook.

## YouTube (Studio → Create → Upload videos)

**Title**
```
What is Verse Arcade? A Bible game where wrong answers teach
```

**Description** (keep the first line exactly; it's the one shown)
```
Play today's verse: https://versearcade.org/play?src=yt-related

One verse a day, the same one for everybody. Read it, answer five quick questions, and when you get one wrong it tells you why. Nobody's ranked against their friends: you only race yourself. And pastors can put Sunday's verse in front of their whole church all week.

0:00 Pop quiz
0:07 The daily verse
0:40 Trivia
0:51 Race the computer
1:03 Your Bible
1:16 Study
1:27 The arcade
1:45 Make it yours
1:59 For pastors
2:19 Play free

It's free: https://versearcade.org/play?src=yt-related
```

- Audience: **No, it's not made for kids**.
- Altered or synthetic content: **No**. That question covers realistic
  footage of people, places or events. This is a screen recording of an
  illustrated app with your own voice.
- Visibility: **Public**.
- After it's live, **pin a comment**: `Play today's verse →
  https://versearcade.org/play?src=yt-comment`

Then, whenever you do the Studio work: Content → Shorts → each Short →
**Related video** → this video. A link can be added to an old Short at any
time.

## Facebook (the Page → Create post → Photo/video)

Upload the **vertical** file as a regular video post, not a Reel, so it can
be pinned.

```
What is Verse Arcade? Here it is in two and a half minutes.

One verse a day, the same one for everybody. Five quick questions about it, and when you get one wrong it tells you why. Nobody's ranked against their friends. And pastors: put Sunday's verse in front of your whole church all week.

It's free. Play today's verse: https://versearcade.org/play?src=fb-pinned
```

Then on the post: **⋯ → Pin to top of Page** (on some Pages it's "Feature"
on the Featured section).
