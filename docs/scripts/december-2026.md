# Recording script — December 1–31

Same two formats, same rules as November (`week-2026-11-09.md`):

- **Two memos per week:** all the quizzes in one, all the myths in the other.
- **Say "Day 1", "Day 2"… before every take**, then pause a second. Day numbers restart each week.
- **Quiz:** read the question, then "Pause it and answer", then **count to three in silence**, then the answer. Say the answer by name, never by letter.
- **Myth:** start on the myth itself, no lead-in. The **bold** word is where the camera cuts tight, so hit it.
- Leave about 3 seconds between takes. Land the last question and stop.

**Christmas week is the one to bank early.** Record weeks 8 and 9 before Dec 20 so nothing depends on you recording on the 24th–27th.

# WEEK 5 · Nov 30 – Dec 6

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 30 · Acts 28** *(already scripted, record it here)*
How does the book of Acts end? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
With Paul in Rome under guard, welcoming everyone who came, for two whole years. No ending. It just stops. Did you know that?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 1 · Daniel 5:5**
At whose feast did a hand appear and write on the wall? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Belshazzar. King of Babylon. That same night his kingdom fell. That's where "the writing on the wall" comes from. Did you get it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 2 · Numbers 22:28**
Whose donkey spoke to him in the Bible? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Balaam. The donkey could see an angel standing in the road. Balaam couldn't. Did you know that one?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 3 · Genesis 5:27**
Who is the oldest person recorded in the Bible? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Methuselah. Nine hundred and sixty-nine. And Noah was his grandson. What did you guess?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 4 · Luke 19:4**
Who climbed a sycamore tree to see Jesus pass by? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Zacchaeus. Jesus stopped right under the tree, looked up, and invited himself to dinner. Did you get it?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 5 · Mark 10:46**
Which blind man kept shouting for Jesus outside Jericho? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Bartimaeus. The crowd told him to be quiet, so he shouted louder. He walked away seeing. Did you know his name?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 6 · Acts 20:9**
A young man dozed off during Paul’s long sermon and fell from a window. Who? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Eutychus. He fell three floors. Paul went down, put his arms around him, and he was alive. Did you get it?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 30 · Luke 15** *(already scripted)*
The Prodigal Son. The word prodigal isn't in the story. Jesus just says, a man had two sons. And the story is just as much about the older one. Did you know that?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 1 · Mark 1:1**
Every gospel tells the Christmas story. **Only** two do. Mark starts with John the Baptist, already grown, out in the wilderness. John starts with: in the beginning was the Word. No manger in either. Did you know two gospels skip it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 2 · Luke 1:13**
The Christmas story starts with Mary. Luke starts **somewhere** else. An old priest named Zechariah, alone in the temple, burning incense. Gabriel comes to him first, six months before Mary. He doesn't believe it, and he can't speak until the baby's born. Did you know who came first?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 3 · Luke 1:34**
Mary just quietly said yes. She did say yes. But **first** she asked a question. How will this be, since I am a virgin? The angel answered her. Then she said, I am the Lord's servant. Did you know she asked?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 4 · Matthew 1:24**
Joseph is right there through the whole Christmas story. Count his lines. There **aren't** any. Not one word of his is recorded. Every time an angel tells him something, he just gets up and does it. Did you notice that?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 5 · Ruth 1:16**
Where you go, I will go. It gets read at weddings all the time. Ruth said it to her **mother-in-law**. Naomi had lost her husband and both sons, and told Ruth to go home. Ruth refused. Was it read at a wedding you went to?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 6 · 1 Corinthians 13:4**
Love is patient, love is kind. We hear it at weddings. Paul wrote it to a church that was **falling** apart. Factions, lawsuits, people arguing over who was most important. He was telling them how to treat each other. Did you know that?

# WEEK 6 · Dec 7–13

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 7 · Daniel 3:25**
How many men did the king see walking in the fiery furnace? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Four. Three were thrown in. The king looked and saw four, walking around in the fire, not burned. What did you say?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 8 · Genesis 41:4**
In Pharaoh’s dream, what ate the seven fat cows? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Seven thin cows. Seven good years, then seven of famine. Joseph read it, and it saved Egypt. Did you get it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 9 · 1 Kings 17:6**
By the brook, what brought Elijah bread and meat? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Ravens. Bread and meat, morning and evening, until the brook ran dry. Did you know that one?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 10 · Acts 12:13**
Peter escaped prison and knocked. Who was so happy she forgot to open the door? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Rhoda. She ran in to tell everyone and left him outside. He kept knocking. Did you get it?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 11 · Acts 16:14**
Which businesswoman, a seller of purple cloth, believed when Paul preached? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Lydia. By a river outside Philippi. And her house became where the church met. Did you know that one?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 12 · Genesis 25:34**
What did Esau sell his birthright for? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
A bowl of stew. Red lentil stew. He was hungry, and that was the price. Did you get it?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 13 · Exodus 3:5**
At the burning bush, what did God tell Moses to remove? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
His sandals. Because the place he was standing on was holy ground. Did you get it?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 7 · Proverbs 13:24**
Spare the rod, spoil the child. People quote it as scripture. It **isn't** in the Bible. It's from an English poem from the sixteen hundreds. Proverbs has a verse near it, and it's worded differently. Who used to say it to you?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 8 · Proverbs 6:16**
The seven deadly sins. Pride, greed, envy, and so on, straight from the Bible. **Not** as a list. That list came later, from church teachers. Proverbs six does have seven things the Lord hates. Haughty eyes, a lying tongue, hands that shed innocent blood. It's a different seven. Could you name all seven?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 9 · Deuteronomy 34:4**
Moses led Israel into the promised land. He led them for forty years, right up to it. Then God took him up a mountain and showed him the whole land. And said, **you will not** cross over. He died there. Joshua took them in. Did you know that?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 10 · Exodus 34:1**
Moses came down the mountain with the Ten Commandments. He did. Then he saw the golden calf and **smashed** them. So God said, cut two more tablets like the first ones, and come back up. There were two sets. Did you know there were two sets?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 11 · Judges 16:19**
Delilah cut Samson's hair. Read it. She got him to fall asleep on her lap, and then she called a man in to shave off the seven braids. She **didn't** do it herself. Who did you picture holding the blade?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 12 · Exodus 2:4**
Baby Moses floated down the Nile alone in a basket. His sister was **watching** from the reeds the whole time. And when Pharaoh's daughter found him, she ran up and offered a nurse. She brought his own mother. Did you know his sister was there?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 13 · Mark 8:9**
Jesus fed the five thousand. Once? **Twice**. Mark tells it again two chapters later. About four thousand people, seven loaves, a few small fish. And afterward Jesus asks them, don't you remember? How many baskets, both times? Did you know about the second one?

# WEEK 7 · Dec 14–20

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 14 · Joshua 10:13**
Whose prayer made the sun stand still? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Joshua. The sun stood still about a whole day, until the battle was done. Did you know that one?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 15 · 2 Kings 5:14**
How many times did Naaman dip in the Jordan to be healed? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Seven. He nearly went home angry. His own servants talked him into it, and his skin came back like a child's. Did you get it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 16 · John 21:11**
After the risen Jesus told them where to cast, how many fish were in the net? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
A hundred and fifty-three. Somebody counted. And the net didn't tear. What did you guess?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 17 · Matthew 26:15**
How many pieces of silver was Judas paid? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Thirty. And later he threw it all back onto the temple floor. Did you get it?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 18 · Acts 9:40**
What was the name of the woman in Joppa Peter raised, known for making clothes for widows? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Tabitha. Also called Dorcas. The widows stood around Peter holding up the clothes she'd made them. Did you know that one?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 19 · Luke 1:63**
Zechariah couldn’t speak, so he wrote his son’s name. What did he write? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
John. Everybody wanted to name him after his father. The second he wrote John, he could talk again. Did you get it?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 20 · Luke 1:40**
After the angel’s visit, which relative did Mary hurry to see? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Elizabeth. She was six months pregnant with John, and when Mary walked in, the baby leaped. Did you know that?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 14 · Matthew 19:24**
The eye of the needle was a small gate in Jerusalem a camel could squeeze through. Nice story. It **isn't** in the Bible, and it shows up centuries later. The disciples weren't comforted. They were astonished, and asked, then who can be saved? Had you heard the gate story?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 15 · Matthew 18:20**
Where two or three are gathered in my name, I am there. We say it at small prayer meetings. Read the verses just before it. It's about settling a **quarrel**. Go to the person who wronged you. If they won't listen, take one or two others. Did you know the context?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 16 · Luke 3:23**
Jesus died at thirty-three. Every sermon says it. The Bible **never** gives that number. Luke says he was about thirty when he began. Thirty-three is worked out by adding up other details. Where did you hear thirty-three?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 17 · Daniel 6:16**
Daniel in the lions' den. Picture a young man. He came to Babylon as a young man. The lions are more than **sixty** years later, after Babylon itself has fallen. He was likely around eighty. Still praying three times a day. How old did you picture him?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 18 · Genesis 17:5**
Abraham. We use the name from the start. He was **Abram**. For ninety-nine years. Then God renamed him Abraham, father of many, when he still had only one son. Did you know his first name?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 19 · Genesis 17:17**
Sarah laughed when God said she'd have a baby. She did. But Abraham laughed **first**, a chapter earlier. He fell on his face and laughed. A hundred years old, and Sarah ninety. And they named the boy Isaac. It means he laughs. Did you know he laughed too?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 20 · Matthew 2:1**
Three kings came to see Jesus. Matthew **never** calls them kings. He calls them magi, from the east. Star watchers. The crowns came later, from carols and paintings. Did you sing We Three Kings?

# WEEK 8 · Dec 21–27

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 21 · Micah 5:2**
Which prophet named Bethlehem as the ruler’s birthplace? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Micah. Hundreds of years before. And when the wise men asked Herod, his scholars quoted it. Did you get it?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 22 · Matthew 1:23**
What does Immanuel mean? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
God with us. Matthew stops the story to translate it, so nobody misses it. Did you know that one?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 23 · Luke 2:1**
Which emperor ordered the census that sent Joseph to Bethlehem? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Caesar Augustus. A decree from Rome, and a pregnant woman walks to Bethlehem. Did you get it?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 24 · Luke 2:4**
Why did Joseph have to go to Bethlehem? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Because he was from the house and line of David, and Bethlehem was David's town. Did you get it?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 25 · Isaiah 9:6**
Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, and what? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Prince of Peace. Isaiah wrote that about seven hundred years before. Merry Christmas. Did you know all four?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 26 · Luke 2:38**
Which old prophet in the temple told everyone about the baby Jesus? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Anna. She worshipped there night and day, and she told everyone who was waiting about him. Did you know her?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 27 · Matthew 2:14**
Where did Joseph take Mary and Jesus to escape Herod? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Egypt. An angel warned Joseph in a dream, and they left that same night. Did you get it?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 21 · Genesis 8:4**
Noah's ark landed on Mount Ararat. Read it again. It says the mountains of Ararat. **Plural**. A whole region. It never names one mountain. Did you know that one?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 22 · Luke 2:9**
Gabriel appeared to the shepherds. Luke names Gabriel twice, for Zechariah and for Mary. For the shepherds, he just says, an angel of the Lord. **No name**. Who did you think it was?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 23 · Luke 2:12**
The shepherds followed the star to Bethlehem. There's **no star** in Luke. The angel gave them a different sign: a baby, wrapped in cloths, lying in a manger. The star is Matthew's, and it's for the magi. Which version was in your nativity play?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 24 · Luke 2:13**
Hark, the herald angels sing. In Luke, they **don't sing**. A great company of angels appears, praising God, and saying: glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace. Saying. Did you ever notice?

**Day 5 · Fri Dec 25 · Luke 2:7**
Little Lord Jesus, no crying he makes. That's a carol. The Bible **never** says it. Luke just says, she wrapped him in cloths and laid him in a manger. A real baby. He probably cried. Merry Christmas. Did you sing that one?

**Day 6 · Sat Dec 26 · Luke 2:25**
An old priest named Simeon held baby Jesus in the temple. Luke **never** calls him a priest. Just a man in Jerusalem, righteous and devout, waiting. God had told him he'd see the Messiah before he died. Did you picture him in robes?

**Day 7 · Sun Dec 27 · Matthew 14:29**
Peter stepped out of the boat and sank. **Not right away**. Matthew says he walked on the water and came toward Jesus. Then he saw the wind, got scared, and started to sink. He walked first. Would you have stepped out?

# WEEK 9 · Dec 28–31 (record before Christmas)

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 28 · Luke 2:46**
How long did Mary and Joseph search before they found 12-year-old Jesus? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Three days. They found him in the temple, sitting with the teachers, asking questions. What did you guess?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 29 · Matthew 3:4**
What did John the Baptist eat in the wilderness? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Locusts and wild honey. Camel hair clothes and a leather belt to go with it. Did you get it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 30 · John 1:29**
When John the Baptist saw Jesus coming, what did he call him? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
The Lamb of God. Who takes away the sin of the world. Did you get it?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 31 · Genesis 1:3**
What did God make on the very first day? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Light. And here's the strange part: the sun doesn't show up until day four. Happy New Year. Did you know that?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Dec 28 · Psalm 46:10**
Be still and know that I am God. We hear it as calm, quiet time. Read the verse before. He makes **wars** cease to the ends of the earth. He breaks the bow and burns the shields. Be still is stop fighting. Did you know what comes before it?

**Day 2 · Tue Dec 29 · Philippians 4:13**
I can do all things through Christ. It's on gym walls and jerseys. Read the verse right before. Paul is in chains, talking about being content whether he's full or **hungry**. That's the all things. Where have you seen it?

**Day 3 · Wed Dec 30 · Lamentations 3:23**
His mercies are new every morning. It sounds like a sunrise verse. It's in **Lamentations**. A whole book of grief over Jerusalem burned to the ground. Right in the middle of the ruins: his mercies never end, they're new every morning. Did you know where it comes from?

**Day 4 · Thu Dec 31 · Jeremiah 29:11**
I know the plans I have for you. It's on graduation cards and coffee mugs. It was a **letter**. Jeremiah sent it to a whole nation carried off to Babylon. Build houses, he said. Settle in. And after seventy years, I will bring you home. Happy New Year. Where have you seen it written?
