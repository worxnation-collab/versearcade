# Recording script — week of Nov 2–8

Two posts a day, both carrying your voice:

- **Morning: Can you answer this?** You ask, the four options appear on
  screen (don't read them), a 3-second pause, then you reveal and teach.
  Questions come straight from the app's own trivia data.
- **Evening: Most people get this wrong.** A common belief, what the text
  actually says, and a question back to the viewer.

## How to record

- **Two memos:** one for all 7 quizzes, one for all 7 myths.
- **Before each take, say "Day 1", "Day 2"… then pause for a second.** That
  is what the cutter dates each take by.
- **Quiz takes have two halves.** Read the QUESTION, stop and count to
  three in silence, then read the ANSWER. One take, one pause. I cut it there
  and the countdown goes in the gap.
- Leave about 3 seconds of silence between takes.
- **Don't read the A/B/C/D letters.** The options are shuffled on screen, so
  always say the answer by name.
- Talk to one person, not a crowd. Same voice as your hooks.

## MEMO 1: Can you answer this? (mornings, 7:00 ET)

### Day 1, Mon Nov 2 · Jonah
**Question:** Most people know how Jonah starts. Almost nobody knows how it
ends. How does the book of Jonah end? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** With a question from God that Jonah never answers. "Should I not
have compassion on that great city?" The book just stops there, and leaves
the question with you. Did you know that?

### Day 2, Tue Nov 3 · Judges
**Question:** God sent Gideon into battle with fewer men on purpose. How many
did he actually take? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** Three hundred. He started with thirty-two thousand, and God kept
cutting it down so nobody could say they saved themselves. They won with
torches, jars and trumpets. What did you guess?

### Day 3, Wed Nov 4 · Genesis
**Question:** Jacob worked seven years to marry the woman he loved, and woke
up married to her sister. What was the sister's name? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** Leah. Her father Laban swapped them on the wedding night, and
Jacob worked seven more years for Rachel. Leah is the one Judah comes from,
and David after him. Did you get it?

### Day 4, Thu Nov 5 · Ruth
**Question:** Ruth was a foreigner who gleaned leftover grain to survive. Her
great-grandson became one of the most famous people in the Bible. Who? Pause
it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** King David. Ruth's son was Obed, Obed's son was Jesse, and Jesse's
son was David. A widow from Moab, picking up what the harvesters left behind.
Did you know that one?

### Day 5, Fri Nov 6 · John
**Question:** What's the shortest verse in most English Bibles? Pause it and
answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** "Jesus wept." John eleven thirty-five, at the tomb of his friend
Lazarus. And here's the thing: he knew he was about to raise him. He wept
anyway. Did you get it?

### Day 6, Sat Nov 7 · Joshua
**Question:** When Israel spied out Jericho, one woman inside the walls hid
the spies on her roof. Who was she? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** Rahab. She hid them under stalks of flax, and her whole family was
spared. Then Matthew puts her name in the family line of Jesus. Did you know
that?

### Day 7, Sun Nov 8 · Luke
**Question:** When Jesus was born, who does Luke say were the first people
told? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
**Answer:** Shepherds. Night-shift workers out in the fields, not priests,
not kings. Luke does this on purpose all the way through his book. What did
you say?

## MEMO 2: Most people get this wrong (evenings)

### Day 1, Mon Nov 2 · Jonah 1:17
Jonah was swallowed by a whale. Except the book of Jonah never says whale. It
says the Lord appointed a great fish. A big fish, sent on purpose, at exactly
the right moment. Which is honestly the stranger part. So what do you think
it was?

### Day 2, Tue Nov 3 · Matthew 2:11
Three wise men at the manger. Matthew never says three. He says there were
three gifts, and we counted the gifts. And they weren't at the manger. Matthew
says they came into the house. Jesus may have been walking by then. Which
part did you grow up with?

### Day 3, Wed Nov 4 · 1 Timothy 6:10
"Money is the root of all evil." That's not what it says. Paul wrote that the
love of money is a root of all kinds of evil. Not the money. The love of it.
One word changes the whole verse. Which version did you learn?

### Day 4, Thu Nov 5 · Genesis 3:6
Adam and Eve ate an apple. Genesis never says apple. It just says fruit. The
apple came much later, mostly from paintings. The fruit was never the point
anyway. What did you always picture?

### Day 5, Fri Nov 6 · Genesis 7:2
Noah took two of every animal onto the ark. Read the next chapter. For the
clean animals, God tells him to take seven pairs. Two is only half the story.
Did you know that one?

### Day 6, Sat Nov 7 · not in the Bible
"God helps those who help themselves." It sounds like scripture. It isn't in
the Bible at all. It's an old proverb. What the Bible says, again and again,
is that God helps the ones who can't. Where did you first hear it?

### Day 7, Sun Nov 8 · Luke 2:7
The innkeeper who turned Mary and Joseph away. There is no innkeeper in the
story. Luke just says there was no room for them. Nobody slams a door. The
whole Christmas play was built around a line that isn't there. Did your
church have one?
