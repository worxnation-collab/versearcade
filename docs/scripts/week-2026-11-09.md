# Recording script — November 9–30

Same two formats and same rules as the first week:

- **Two memos per week:** all the quizzes in one, all the myths in the other.
- **Say "Day 1", "Day 2"… before every take**, then pause a second.
- **Quiz:** read the question, then "Pause it and answer", then **count to three
  in silence**, then the answer. Say the answer by name, never by letter.
- Leave about 3 seconds between takes.

Day numbers restart each week.

---

# WEEK 2 · Nov 9–15

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 9 · 1 Samuel 25**
A woman stopped David from taking revenge with nothing but food. Who was she?
Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Abigail. She rode out to meet him with loaves, wine and figs, and talked him
out of it. Did you get it?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 10 · Judges 4**
An enemy general ran from battle and hid in what he thought was a friendly
tent. Who took him down? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Jael. With a tent peg. Deborah sings about it a chapter later. Did you know
that one?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 11 · John 3**
One religious leader came to Jesus at night so nobody would see. Who was he?
Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Nicodemus. And he shows up twice more. The last time, he's carrying spices to
the tomb. Did you get it?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 12 · Acts 7**
Who was the first follower of Jesus to die for it? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Stephen. And while they stoned him, a young man named Saul stood there holding
their coats. That's Paul. Did you know that?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 13 · 1 Samuel 3**
A boy heard his name called three times in the night and kept running to the
wrong person. Who was the boy? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Samuel. He thought it was Eli the priest. The third time, Eli told him to say,
"Speak, for your servant is listening." What did you guess?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 14 · Esther 2**
Esther was an orphan. Who raised her? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Her cousin Mordecai. He took her in when her parents died, and later he's the
one who tells her she might be there "for such a time as this." Did you get
it?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 15 · Judges 6**
Gideon asked God for a sign. Twice. What did he use? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
A wool fleece. First wet with the ground dry, then dry with the ground wet.
And God answered both times. Did you know that one?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 9 · Acts 9:4**
Paul got knocked off his horse on the road to Damascus. Read it. There's no
horse. Acts just says he fell to the ground. Every painting added the horse.
Did you picture one too?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 10 · Matthew 14:21**
Jesus fed five thousand people. Matthew says five thousand MEN, besides women
and children. The real crowd was a lot bigger. How many do you think were
there?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 11 · Genesis 4:15**
The mark of Cain was a curse. Read it again. God put the mark on him so nobody
would kill him. It was protection, given to the first murderer. Did you know
that?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 12 · Jonah 4:2**
Jonah ran because he was scared of Nineveh. That's not what he says. He says
he ran because he knew God was gracious and would forgive them. He ran from
mercy. Did that surprise you?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 13 · Acts 13:9**
God changed Saul's name to Paul on the road to Damascus. He didn't. He's still
called Saul for years after. Then one day Luke just writes, "Saul, who was
also called Paul." That's it. Did you think it changed at his conversion?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 14 · Isaiah 11:6**
The lion will lie down with the lamb. Isaiah never says that. He says the wolf
will live with the lamb, and the leopard will lie down with the goat. The
lion's in there, with a calf. Which version did you learn?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 15 · Proverbs 16:18**
Pride comes before a fall. Close, but the verse is: pride goes before
destruction, a haughty spirit before a fall. Destruction comes first. Did you
know there were two halves?

# WEEK 3 · Nov 16–22

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 16 · Jonah 1**
God sent Jonah east to Nineveh. Where did Jonah try to sail instead? Pause it
and answer.
*(count to three)*
Tarshish. As far the other way as a ship could take him. And he was asleep
below deck when the storm hit. Did you get it?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 17 · John 4**
Jesus' longest recorded conversation with anyone was with a woman at a well.
Where was the well? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Samaria. A Samaritan woman, at noon, alone. And she went back and told the
whole town. Did you know that?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 18 · Matthew 9**
What was Matthew's job when Jesus called him? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Tax collector. He got up and left the booth, and the next scene is a dinner at
his house full of the wrong sort of people. Did you get it?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 19 · Acts 11**
Followers of Jesus were first called Christians in one city. Which one? Pause
it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Antioch. Not Jerusalem. And it seems to have started as a nickname outsiders
gave them. What did you guess?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 20 · 1 Kings 10**
A queen travelled a long way just to test Solomon with hard questions. Who was
she? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
The Queen of Sheba. She brought gold and spices, and said she hadn't been told
the half of it. Did you know that one?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 21 · Genesis 11**
At one tower, the whole world's single language became many. Which tower?
Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Babel. They built it to reach the heavens and make a name for themselves.
Did you get it?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 22 · Exodus 32**
While Moses was up the mountain getting the commandments, what were the
people making at the bottom? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
A golden calf, out of their own earrings. Moses came down and broke the
tablets. Did you know that?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 16 · Luke 10:33**
The Good Samaritan. Jesus never calls him good. The story just says, a
Samaritan. "Good" is something we added, maybe because we didn't expect it.
Did you know that one?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 17 · John 11:16**
Doubting Thomas. Remember what else he said. When Jesus decided to go back
toward danger, Thomas said, let's go too, so we can die with him. Did you
know Thomas said that?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 18 · Genesis 8:14**
Noah was on the ark for forty days. It rained for forty days. They were on the
ark for about a year. Which number did you grow up with?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 19 · Genesis 2:17**
Adam and Eve ate from the tree of life. That's the other tree. The forbidden
one was the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. Both were in the garden.
Did you mix them up too?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 20 · not in the Bible**
"Cleanliness is next to godliness." Not in the Bible. Not anywhere in it. It's
an old saying that got attached to scripture. Who used to say it to you?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 21 · not in the Bible**
"This too shall pass." It sounds like Solomon. It isn't in the Bible. It's an
old Persian saying. What verse do you hold on to instead?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 22 · not in the Bible**
"To thine own self be true." People quote it like scripture. It's Shakespeare.
Hamlet. Did you think it was in the Bible?

# WEEK 4 · Nov 23–29

## Quizzes (mornings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 23 · 1 Samuel 1**
A woman prayed for a son so silently that the priest thought she was drunk.
Who was she? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Hannah. Her son was Samuel, and she gave him back to God. Did you get it?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 24 · Colossians 4:14**
What was Luke's job? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
A doctor. Paul calls him the beloved physician. And he wrote more of the New
Testament than most people realise: Luke and Acts. Did you know that?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 25 · Jeremiah 29**
Jeremiah told the exiles in Babylon how long they'd be there. How many years?
Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Seventy. And years later, Daniel is reading that exact number and working out
that the time is nearly up. Did you get it?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 26 · Luke 24**
Two disciples walked with the risen Jesus all afternoon without recognising
him. On which road? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
The road to Emmaus. They knew him when he broke the bread at the table.
Happy Thanksgiving. Did you get it?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 27 · Esther 4**
Before Esther walked in to see the king, she asked her people to do one thing
for three days. What was it? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Fast. Going in unsummoned could cost her life. She said, "If I perish, I
perish." Did you know that one?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 28 · John 2**
What was Jesus' first miracle in John's gospel? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
Water into wine, at a wedding in Cana. Six stone jars, and the best wine
saved for last. Did you get it?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 29 · John 13**
At the last supper, Jesus did a job that belonged to the lowest servant in the
house. What was it? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
He washed their feet. Peter tried to refuse. Did you get it?

## Myths (evenings)

**Day 1 · Mon Nov 23 · Luke 2:10**
Angels are sweet little babies with wings. Every time one shows up in the
Bible, the first thing it says is, do not be afraid. Nobody says that about a
baby. What did you picture?

**Day 2 · Tue Nov 24 · 2 Kings 2:11**
Elijah died. The Bible never says he did. A chariot of fire, a whirlwind, and
he was taken up. That's the last we see of him. Did you know that?

**Day 3 · Wed Nov 25 · Genesis 3:1**
Satan tempted Eve in the garden. Genesis 3 never uses that name. It says the
serpent. The connection gets made later, in Revelation. Did you know that one?

**Day 4 · Thu Nov 26 · 1 Corinthians 10:13**
God won't give you more than you can handle. That's not quite the verse. It's
about temptation: God won't let you be tempted beyond what you can bear, and
he'll give you a way out. Happy Thanksgiving. Which version did you hear?

**Day 5 · Fri Nov 27 · Matthew 2:1**
The wise men followed the star straight to Bethlehem. They went to Jerusalem
first and asked King Herod where the baby was. That's how Herod found out.
Did you know that part?

**Day 6 · Sat Nov 28 · Luke 2:5**
Mary rode a donkey to Bethlehem. Luke never mentions a donkey. He just says
they went. Every Christmas card added it. Did yours have one?

**Day 7 · Sun Nov 29 · Luke 2**
Jesus was born on December twenty-fifth. The Bible never gives a date. Not a
month, not a day. The date came centuries later. Did you know that?

# Mon Nov 30 (record it with week 5)

**Quiz · Acts 28**
How does the book of Acts end? Pause it and answer.
*(count to three)*
With Paul in Rome under guard, welcoming everyone who came, for two whole
years. No ending. It just stops. Did you know that?

**Myth · Luke 15**
The Prodigal Son. The word prodigal isn't in the story. Jesus just says, a man
had two sons. And the story is just as much about the older one. Did you know
that?
