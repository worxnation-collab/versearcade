Verse Arcade

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

Verse Arcade ("we", "us", the "app") is a Bible-learning game. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We built the app to be usable without ever creating an account, and we do not sell your data or use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

Who we are / contact

Verse Arcade is operated by the developer of record listed on the App Store. For any privacy request, email worxnation@gmail.com.

What we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email addressOnly if you create an account (email, Google, or Apple sign-in)To authenticate you and sync your progress across devices
Display name / username & avatar emojiYou choose it at sign-upShown to you and, if you join a group, to that group's members

Guest play collects no personal data. If you play without an account, your progress is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

What we do NOT collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no cross-app tracking (no App Tracking Transparency prompt because we don't track).
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No purchase of data from, or sale of data to, third parties.

How your data is stored

Accounts and gameplay data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase (which hosts on cloud infrastructure with encryption in transit and at rest). Access is restricted by row-level security so you can only read/write your own data (and shared group data for groups you join). See Supabase's privacy policy.

Sign in with Google / Apple

If you use social sign-in, the provider tells us a unique ID and (unless you hide it) your email address, solely to create your account. We do not receive your password or post anything on your behalf. See Google's and Apple's respective privacy policies for how they handle the sign-in.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we may send an optional daily reminder that a new verse is live. You can turn these off any time in your device settings or in the app's Settings screen.

Children's privacy

Verse Arcade is intended for users age 12 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, directly in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account, gameplay data, and group memberships from our backend. You may also email worxnation@gmail.com to request deletion.

Data retention

We keep account and gameplay data until you delete your account. Guest data lives only on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., EEA/UK GDPR, California CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. Account deletion in-app satisfies the deletion right immediately; for anything else, email us.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
