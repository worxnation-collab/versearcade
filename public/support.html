Common questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play every day as a guest — your streak and progress are saved on your device. Creating a free account (email, Google, or Apple) lets you sync across devices and never lose your streak.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app → Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all data. You can also email us to request deletion.

How do streaks work?

Play today's verse each day to keep your streak alive. Miss a day and a streak freeze is used automatically if you have one, so a single busy day won't reset you to zero.

The sound or vibration is too much.

Open Settings in the app and turn Sound and/or Haptics down or off. Your choice is remembered.

How do co-op groups work?

Create or join a group, and everyone plays the same daily verse against a shared clock, earning points together — never ranked against each other.
