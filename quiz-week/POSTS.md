# A week of group quiz posts

Post the card with the caption. That evening, reply to your own post with the answer.
No links in the post itself.

## 1. Fri 9/25: Philemon 1:5 (card 1)

**Caption:**
Paul wrote this about his friend Philemon. Where was Paul when he wrote it? Guess before you look it up 🙂 Answer tonight.

**Answer reply:**
✅ **B: In prison.** He opens the letter calling himself "a prisoner of Christ Jesus" (Philemon 1:1). He was in chains, and he spent the first lines thanking God for somebody else's faith. The whole letter is one chapter, 25 verses, and it's worth five minutes today.

## 2. Sat 9/26: Psalm 46:1 (card 2)

**Caption:**
Most of us have sung this psalm without knowing it. Which hymn came from it? 🎶

**Answer reply:**
✅ **C: A Mighty Fortress Is Our God.** Martin Luther built it on Psalm 46. Bonus fact: this psalm is credited to the sons of Korah. Korah rebelled against Moses in Numbers 16, but his line didn't die out (Numbers 26:11), and his descendants became the temple's singers. The family of a rebel ended up writing "God is our refuge." 🙌

## 3. Sun 9/27: Acts 20:35 (card 3)

**Caption:**
Everybody knows this saying. Almost nobody knows where it's written. Which book is the ONLY one that records it? 🤔

**Answer reply:**
✅ **D: Acts.** "It is more blessed to give than to receive" is Jesus's own teaching, but it doesn't appear in Matthew, Mark, Luke or John. Paul quotes it in Acts 20:35, saying goodbye to the church leaders from Ephesus. Right after, they all knelt and prayed together, and they wept.

## 4. Mon 9/28: John 4:24 (card 4)

**Caption:**
Jesus said this to just one person. Who was it? 👇

**Answer reply:**
✅ **B: A Samaritan woman at a well.** It was Jacob's well, at noon. She asked which mountain was the right place to worship, and Jesus said worship isn't about the mountain. A few verses later He tells her plainly, "I who speak to you am He" (John 4:26). It's one of the first times He says outright who He is, and He said it to an outsider.

---

## 5. Tue 9/29: 1 Timothy 4:12 (card 5)

**Caption:**
Fill in the blank. How fast can you get this one? ⏱️

**Answer reply:**
✅ **C: youth.** Paul wrote this to Timothy, his "son in the faith." He named five places to set the example: speech, conduct, love, faith and purity. None of them depend on how old you are. 💛 Tag a young believer who needs this today.

---

## 6. Wed 9/30: Deuteronomy 31:6 (card 6)

**Caption:**
Most people guess wrong on this one. Who said it FIRST? 👀

**Answer reply:**
✅ **B: Moses.** Most people say Joshua, and that's a good guess, because God says it to Joshua in Joshua 1. But Moses said it first, near the end of his life, to all of Israel before they crossed the Jordan. Then he turned and said it again to Joshua (Deut 31:7–8). Hebrews 13:5 carries the promise to us: "I will never leave you nor forsake you."

---

## 7. Thu 10/1: Hosea 11:4 (card 7)

**Caption:**
One of the most tender verses in the whole Bible. Who's speaking, and about whom? 💬

**Answer reply:**
✅ **B: God, about Israel.** Just before this, God remembers teaching Ephraim to walk and taking them up in His arms (Hosea 11:3), like a parent with a toddler. "Bent down to feed them" is the King of the universe stooping to a child's height. If you guessed Hosea and his wife, that's a fair guess, since his marriage is the book's other big picture. This chapter is God's own voice.
